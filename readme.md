# ⚡ Instant QR Generator(LIVE)
A fast, lightweight, and browser-based tool to generate high-quality QR codes for your URLs. No data collection, no external servers—just pure speed.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [Try it here](https://ahsanjuly29.github.io/QRCodeGenerator/)

---

### 🌟 Why use this?
1. **Real-time Generation**: Watch the QR code change instantly as you type.
2. **Privacy First**: Everything happens in your browser. Your data stays with you.
3. **High-Quality Downloads**: Save your QR as a PNG image for printing or digital marketing.
4. **Mobile Responsive**: Works perfectly on your phone, tablet, or desktop.

---

### 🛠️ Built With
1. **HTML5 & Bootstrap 5**: For a modern and clean user interface.
2. **JavaScript & QRCode.js**: Powering the dynamic client-side generation.

---

### 📖 How to Use
1. Paste your link in the input field.
2. The QR code generates automatically.
3. Click **Download** to save your PNG.
